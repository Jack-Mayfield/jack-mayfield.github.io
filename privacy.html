Privacy

Effective 30 September 2026

Fret.fm is a guitar practice app. This page says what it collects, where that goes, and how to remove it.

Practice data

What you play and log in the app is stored on your device: reps, tempos, the lessons you open, and what you report about your own playing. When you are online, this practice data may be sent to Fret.fm's servers to keep your progress and to improve how the app sequences practice for everyone.

Microphone and audio

The app uses your microphone when a lesson or tool is listening, and it says so on screen while it does. Audio from the microphone, including your playing and anything you say, may be sent to cloud services for speech recognition, for coaching, and for analysis that improves the app. Recordings may be kept for those purposes.

The services that process audio and text on Fret.fm's behalf are, by category: speech and voice services, AI model providers, and cloud hosting. Each processes data only to provide its service to Fret.fm.

What Fret.fm does not do

  • No advertising, and no sale of your data.
  • No tracking across other apps or sites.

Removing your data

Data on your device is removed by clearing the site's data in your browser, or by deleting the installed app. To have data removed from Fret.fm's servers, write to Fret.fm at 1112 North Sheppard Street, Richmond, VA 23230.
